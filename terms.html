1. Who we are

LEGIT-TT is a seller certification service run by Resolut Global (“we”, “us”) in Trinidad and Tobago. We check online sellers on platforms such as Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, and give approved sellers a badge with a unique certificate number and QR code.

2. What certification means

A LEGIT-TT badge means that, at the time of approval, we compared the seller’s identity documents with the applicant, confirmed the seller controls the online accounts listed, and (for Standard and Premium) checked a business registration document. We repeat checks when a badge is renewed.

A badge is not a guarantee. We do not guarantee the quality, safety, legality or delivery of any product or service, and we are not a party to any sale between a buyer and a seller. Buyers should still use safe payment practices.

3. For buyers

  • The status shown on the Verify page is read live. If it says anything other than ACTIVE, do not rely on the badge.
  • “No record found” only means we have no record. It does not mean a seller is dishonest.
  • Use the “Report a fake badge” link to tell us about fake badges or complaints. We review reports but cannot promise any particular outcome.

  • You must tell us promptly if your business details or online accounts change.
