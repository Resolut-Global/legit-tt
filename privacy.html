Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

1. What this covers

This explains what personal information LEGIT-TT, a Resolut Global service, collects, why, who can see it, and your choices. We aim to handle personal data in line with the Data Protection Act of Trinidad and Tobago. [Lawyer to confirm which provisions currently apply.]

2. Buyers and visitors

  • You do not need an account to verify a seller. We do not ask for your name or contact details to verify.
  • This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • When a badge QR code is scanned, we add one to a counter for that seller. We do not record who scanned it.
  • If you report a fake badge or make a complaint, you may give your name and e-mail address (both optional). We use them only to acknowledge and investigate your report.

3. Sellers who apply

We collect: your full name; business name; e-mail address and phone numbers; business category; links to your online profiles; a copy of your identification, a photo of you, proof of address, and (for Standard and Premium) your business registration document; any fraud history you declare; and your payment reference.

We use this only to decide your application, to issue and manage your certification, to contact you, to handle complaints, and to meet legal obligations.

4. What the public can see

For a certified seller the public can see only: business name, category, the platform links you chose to list, certificate number, certification tier and registered-business marker, status, and issue and expiry dates. We never show identification numbers, addresses, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, documents or our review notes.

5. Who can see your documents

Documents and application details are stored in a private Google account and are accessible only to authorised LEGIT-TT administrators. Our systems run on Google services (Google Sheets, Drive and Gmail), so your information is stored and processed by Google on our behalf, which may be outside Trinidad and Tobago. We do not sell personal information.

6. How long we keep it

We keep application records and documents for as long as needed to manage your certification, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations, and after that only as the law requires. [State your retention period.]

7. Your rights

You may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it where the law allows. Contact us at the address below. Certification records that must be kept for verification and dispute purposes may need to remain.

8. Age

Certification is for people aged 18 and over, or businesses acting through an adult.
