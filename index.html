What certification means

Real checks behind every shield

  • Identity checked. We compare the applicant’s ID with a photo of them.
  • Account ownership proven. The seller shows they control the Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp account they list.
  • Business registration checked for Standard and Premium sellers, shown as “Registered Business”.
  • Checked again every year. Badges expire after 12 months, and we can suspend or withdraw one at any time.

LEGIT-TT confirms who a seller is. It does not guarantee any product or service, so always use safe payment practices.
